# Server rules

## Be kind
No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks. Critique the work, not the person.

## Self-promotion
You may share your own work in #showcase. Links to paid products or commissions go only in #self-promo, once a week at most.

## AI-generated art
AI-generated images are allowed in #ai-experiments only, and must be labelled as AI-generated. They are not allowed in the monthly challenge.

## Reporting a problem
To report a member or a message, use /ticket with a short description, or message a moderator. Reports are private.
