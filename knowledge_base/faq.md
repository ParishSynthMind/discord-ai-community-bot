# FAQ

## Can I use the asset packs commercially?
Yes. Asset packs from the club can be used in commercial projects, but you may not resell or redistribute the raw files.

## Do you offer student discounts?
Yes, students get 50 percent off the yearly Premium plan. Open a /ticket with proof of enrolment and a moderator will send a discount code.

## How do I contact the team about sponsorships or partnerships?
Use /contact with your email and a short message. The team replies within two working days.

## Refunds
Monthly payments are not refunded once the month has started. Yearly plans can be refunded within 14 days of payment. Ask for a refund through /ticket.
