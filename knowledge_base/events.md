# Events

## Thursday review call
Every Thursday at 18:00 UTC in the Stage channel. Premium members can book one portfolio review a month; free members can listen.

## Monthly challenge
A new theme is posted on the 1st of each month in #challenge. Entries close on the 25th. The winner gets a free month of Premium and a feature in the newsletter.

## Booking a one-to-one call
Use /book to pick a time with the team for a one-to-one intro call (20 minutes, free). The link is tied to your Discord account.
