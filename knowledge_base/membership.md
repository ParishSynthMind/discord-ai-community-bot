# Membership

## What Premium includes
Premium members get the #premium-lounge and #portfolio-reviews channels, one live portfolio review a month in the Thursday review call, early access to new asset packs two weeks before everyone else, and a Premium role colour. Everything in the free channels stays free.

## Price and billing
Premium costs 9 US dollars a month or 90 US dollars a year. You pay through the checkout link the bot gives you with /premium. The link is tied to your Discord account, so the Premium role arrives by itself within a minute of paying. There is no need to send a receipt to a moderator.

## Cancelling
You can cancel at any time from the billing portal link in your payment receipt email. Premium stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for, then the role is removed automatically.

## If a payment fails
If a renewal payment fails, Premium stays on for 3 days while the card is retried. Updating your card in the billing portal keeps it on. After 3 days without a successful payment the Premium role is removed; paying again brings it back.

## Role missing after paying
Run /premium. If your payment went through, the bot puts the role back straight away. If it still says you do not have Premium, open a /ticket and a moderator will look at it.
